All your music, one player.

AlexLMP 2.8.2 brings a new PLUS style, a new player, big artwork, smoother playback and a lot of fixes.

## New since 2.8.0

**New looks**
- PLUS: AlexLMP's own new app style and player. A tile-grid Home (Now Playing, Liked, Stats, Shuffle all, Search), numbered top picks, and a player with a barcode-style seek bar and a play button that morphs between a rounded square and a circle. Pick it in Settings > Appearance > App style.
- New "AlexLMP" player: connected buttons that stretch and squish each other when you press them, a play button that changes shape when you play or pause, and colors that blend into each new song.
- Big artwork: double-tap the artwork to make it fill the top of the player edge to edge. Double-tap again to go back. Works in AlexLMP Classic, AlexLMP and PLUS.
- The old AlexLMP player is now called AlexLMP Classic and is still the default.
- A button in the lyrics screen to show or hide lyrics on the player artwork. Lyrics on the artwork are now off until you turn them on.

**Smoother**
- Preloading the next song is much more reliable: it retries after network hiccups, works in shuffle, and tries again halfway through each song.
- Crossfade no longer stutters before the blend, and the loudness stays even through the fade.
- No more flicker when you slightly swipe the player up or down.
- Swiping home from the player no longer opens the queue by accident.
- Prestige Home lag fixed.

**Fixes**
- Media controls from other apps (like crDroid's dynamic bar or custom players) now work reliably and no longer randomly stop responding.
- The home screen widget no longer shows "Nothing playing" in the middle of a song.
- With crossfade on, a song you queued in Up next could get skipped. Fixed.
- The app icon now fills your launcher's shape properly instead of showing a circle inside a square.
- Fewer pop-ups: confirmation messages are gone, only real problems are shown.

**Removed**
- The Prestige player and vinyl mode. The Prestige app style is still there.

## Heads up
- The music notification now stays while paused so other apps can keep controlling playback. Tap its stop button to close it.
- After updating, the AlexLMP icon may disappear from your home screen once. Add it back from the app drawer.

Updating installs over your current version. Your library, likes and settings stay.

If you enjoy AlexLMP, you can support the project from the card at the top of Settings <3
