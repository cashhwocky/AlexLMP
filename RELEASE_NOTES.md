AlexLMP 3.2.1 gives the AI tab a full redesign and makes search much nicer.

## New
- **AI DJ redone.** Pure AMOLED black background whenever you open it, a ChatGPT-style chat layout, haptic feedback as it answers, and glowing mood colors that shift when you send a message.
- **Search results redone.** New "All" view with a Top result card, rounded song groups, and scrolling Artists, Albums and Playlists rows, plus rounded filter pills.

## From 3.2.0
AlexLMP 3.2.0 puts the new tab bar and AI DJ in every app style, and redoes the Bloom player, library and settings.

## New
- **Floating tab bar + AI DJ in every app style** (Bloom, Signal, Apple, Prestige, Plus, Pocket and the default look). Home, Mix, Library, AI, Search and Settings now live in the same rounded dock, colored to match each style.
- **Bloom player redone.** No more gradient or glow: a clean flat background in your theme color, a rounded transport dock for shuffle / previous / play / next / repeat, and a dock-style row for Lyrics, Up next, Sound, Sleep and Share.
- **Bloom library redone.** Sliding Playlists / Albums / Songs switch, new Downloaded and Shuffle all shortcuts, and softer rounded cards and lists.
- **Settings redone.** New header with back button and version, icon badges on each section, and rounded bordered cards.

## From 3.1.1

## Smoother Bloom
- **Home:** pill-style switch between YouTube Music and your phone with a sliding highlight, softer rounded cards and song lists, and blocks that glide in when you open it.
- **Mini player:** now a floating pill that lines up with the tab bar.
- **Full player:** the play button morphs smoothly between a circle and a rounded square, round skip buttons, and the Lyrics / Up next / Sound / Sleep / Share buttons sit in a dock-style pill.

## From 3.1.0
AI DJ and a new bottom tab bar.

## New
- **AI DJ tab.** Chat with an assistant that can control your music. Say "play Blinding Lights by The Weeknd" and it searches the app (your songs first, then YouTube Music) and plays it. It can also queue songs, skip, go back, pause, resume and shuffle.
- **New floating tab bar** (Home, Search, Library, AI, Settings), matching bitFocus.

## Fixed
- **No more smeared block under your playlists.** The blurred artwork at the top of a playlist was spilling past its edge and showing up as a slanted, smeared shape under the song list. It now stays at the top and fades out cleanly, there and everywhere else that background is used.

- **New app icon** in Material You lilac to match your other icons.
- **Big playlists and Liked songs no longer lag.**
