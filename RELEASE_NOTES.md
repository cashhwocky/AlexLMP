All your music, one player.

AlexLMP 2.9.0 is a whole new look: AlexLMP Signal.

## New look
- **AlexLMP Signal is the new default** for both the app and the player. It has a near-black background, one accent color and bold new headings. The AlexLMP logo bars run through the whole app: they mark the song that's playing, the start of each section and the song scrubber.
- **One floating dock.** The mini player and the tabs (Home, Search, Library, Settings) now sit together at the bottom. Swipe the mini player left or right to change songs, or swipe it up to open the player.
- **New Home.** Jump back in, On repeat, your mixes and your playlists, with one tap to switch between YouTube Music and the music on your phone.
- **New player.** Big artwork with a soft glow in the song's colors, a scrubber made of the logo bars, a big play button, and an Up next card (tap it or swipe it up for the queue). Lyrics, Queue, Sound and Sleep sit right below. Swipe down to close, swipe the artwork to change songs and double-tap for big artwork, same as before.
- **Nothing was taken away.** Every other app style and player style is still in Settings > Appearance, and all your settings stay the same.

## Also new
- **Better search results.** A Top result card at the top (the artist if you searched their name, otherwise the best song) and cleaner song rows.
- **See all on artist pages.** Top songs and Albums both have a See all button that opens the artist's full list.
- **Spotify imports in your YouTube Music library.** Playlists you imported from Spotify now show up in the YouTube Music library too, right after Downloaded.

Updating installs over your current version. Your library, likes and settings stay.

If you enjoy AlexLMP, you can support the project from the card at the top of Settings <3
