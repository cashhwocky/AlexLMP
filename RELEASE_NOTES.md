All your music, one player.

AlexLMP 2.8.6 adds three things you asked for.

## New
- **Like from the notification.** The music notification now has a heart button, the same heart the players use. Tap it to like or unlike the song. It stays in sync if you like the song inside the app.
- **Import Spotify playlists.** Settings > Library > Import Spotify playlist. Export a playlist as a CSV (for example with Exportify), pick the file, and AlexLMP finds each song on YouTube Music and saves them as a playlist. Songs it can't find are listed at the end so you can add them by hand.
- **Report a bug.** Settings > About > Report a bug. It copies your app version, phone and the last error, then opens the AlexLMP Telegram chat so you can paste it in.

## Also includes 2.8.5
- Songs YouTube only allows for signed-in accounts no longer show a giant error. AlexLMP now says clearly that it can't play them yet.

Updating installs over your current version. Your library, likes and settings stay.

If you enjoy AlexLMP, you can support the project from the card at the top of Settings <3
