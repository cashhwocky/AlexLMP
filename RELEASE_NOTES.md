All your music, one player.

AlexLMP 3.0.1 makes Bloom smooth with big libraries.

## Fixed
- **Big playlists and Liked songs no longer lag.** Pages with hundreds or thousands of songs (like Liked on YouTube) now only build the songs on screen, so they open instantly and scroll smoothly.
- **Library's Songs list** loads the same way, so it stays fast however many songs you have.

## AlexLMP Bloom (3.0)
- A fresh Material You design with soft lilac colors, scalloped cookie shapes, flower buttons and wavy progress bars. New Home, Search tab, Library, album, playlist and artist pages, and the Bloom player. Every other theme and style is still in Settings > Appearance.

Updating installs over your current version. Your library, likes and settings stay.

If you enjoy AlexLMP, you can support the project from the card at the top of Settings <3
