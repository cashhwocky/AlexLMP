All your music, one player.

AlexLMP 3.0 is a whole new look: AlexLMP Bloom.

## New
- **AlexLMP Bloom.** A fresh Material You design with soft lilac colors, scalloped cookie shapes, flower buttons and wavy progress bars. It's the new default look, and it isn't true black (AMOLED) anymore.
- **New Home.** A widget-style top with Jump back in, Liked songs and Discover, then Quick picks, Mixed for you, Listen again and more.
- **New Search tab.** A big search bar, your recent searches, and Browse tiles to find playlists by mood or genre. Results show a top result, songs, albums, playlists and artists together, with filters for each, plus what's on your phone.
- **New Library.** Playlists, Albums and Songs filters, a big Liked songs card, and a grid of your playlists and albums in different shapes (or a list, if you prefer).
- **New album, playlist and artist pages** in the Bloom style.
- **Bloom player.** A big cookie-shaped play button, a moving wavy seek bar, a flower like button, and Lyrics, Up next, Sound, Sleep and Share right under the controls.
- **Lilac theme.** The new default color. Every other theme and all the old app and player styles are still in Settings.

Updating installs over your current version. Your library, likes and settings stay. You'll switch to Bloom once, and you can switch back in Settings > Appearance.

If you enjoy AlexLMP, you can support the project from the card at the top of Settings <3
