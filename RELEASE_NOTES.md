bitLMP 3.8.2 brings back the Bloom icon as the default.

## Changed
- **Bloom is the default app icon again.** The lilac Bloom icon is back as the default. If you picked a different icon, you keep it.
- **New pixel icon to pick.** "BIT" over "LMP" in pixel letters with a little cursor, in Settings > App icon.

## From 3.8.0
- AlexLMP is now **bitLMP**, joining the bit family of apps (bitMessages, bitDialer, bitMaps and more).
- **New name: bitLMP.** Same app, same features, new name. Your library, likes, playlists and settings all stay, and updates keep installing over your current version.
- **New app icons.** Every app icon is redesigned with the new bitLMP mark: a disc with three sound bars. The default is a white disc with purple bars on black, matching the other bit apps. All the other icon colors are still in Settings > App icon with the new mark.
- **Updates you won't miss.** When a new version is out, a full-screen update page shows what's new every time you open the app, with one tap to update.

## From 3.7.6
- **Music controls work again.** Play could do nothing when the song from last time had expired or failed to load, or a fade got cut off and left the song silent. Play now reloads the song (at the same spot) or brings the volume back, so you don't need to reset your settings anymore.
- **No more lag when switching tabs.** Tabs you've opened stay loaded, so going back to Home, Search, Library or AI is instant and keeps your place.

## From 3.7.5
- **Normal bitLMP is back.** Thanks for all the feedback on the Liquid Glass versions. The app and the player are back to how they were in 3.6.3, and all the app styles and player styles are back too. If you were moved to Liquid Glass, you're switched back automatically.
- **Smoother tab bar.** The tab bar has a new pill that marks your tab. Drag along the bar and the pill follows your finger with a soft ripple, then settles on the nearest tab. It uses your theme colors.
- **Borealis is the new default app icon.** If you picked a different icon before, you keep it. You can switch any time in Settings > App icon.
- **AI stays.** The AI tab, AI DJ and everything else from 3.6 are all still here.

## From 3.6.3
- **Every album and single, newest first.** "See all" on an artist's Albums or Singles now lists everything they have out on YouTube Music, straight from the artist's own page, sorted from newest to oldest.
- **Exact release dates.** Album and single pages show the full date it came out (like "Released March 15, 2024"), not just the year.
- **The AI DJ sounds like a real DJ.** No more cheesy "good morning sunshine" lines. It's calm and talks about the music.

## From 3.6.2
- **Starting the DJ from Home works again.** It opened the "Add music" menu instead of the player when you only use YouTube Music.
- **The DJ screen shows up every time the DJ talks,** not only when you start it from the player. Wherever you are in the app, the player comes up with the DJ on screen.
- **No more crackly audio when the song comes back in.** The song now fades in with one smooth fade.

## From 3.6.1
- **The DJ screen looks right.** No more yellow underlines or typewriter font, and it's fully solid instead of see-through.
- **Starting the DJ opens the player** with the DJ screen up straight away while it gets your set ready. When it finishes talking, the screen fades away and the song slowly fades in.
- **New AI DJ button** in the Bloom colors, with a cleaner DJ icon.

## From 3.6.0
- **A real AI brain.** The AI now remembers the whole conversation, uses your own AI key and model from Settings > AI, waits long enough for proper answers, and tries a backup service before giving up. If it still can't connect, it shows the exact error.
- **It knows your taste.** Every answer, mix and search pick takes into account what's playing, what's up next, what you've played lately, your liked songs, your favorite artists and your playlists.
- **The AI can run the app.** Just ask: play or queue songs, build and play a mix, save a whole new playlist to your library, like or unlike the song, skip, pause, shuffle, repeat, set a sleep timer, change the volume, or start and stop the AI DJ. It can do a few of these in one go.
- **Smarter search and song info.** AI search understands moods and typos and picks real songs for you. "Ask AI" in the player explains what a song is about and suggests similar ones.
- **A smarter AI DJ.** It talks about the actual artists and songs it's about to play, plans the set so it flows, keeps adding AI picks that fit, and if you skip two songs fast it notices and switches direction.
- **The DJ takes over the player while it talks.** A big moving voice, the words appearing as they're spoken, and "Tap to skip the talk". The set starts right when the DJ finishes speaking.
- The "Spotify AI DJ" label is gone. It's just the AI DJ.
