All your music, one player. Now on Linux.

AlexLMP for Linux 0.1.0 is the first desktop version.

## What's here
- **The AlexLMP look, made for a computer.** A sidebar with Home, Search, Mix, Library, Settings and your playlists. Your music in the middle, and a player bar along the bottom with the song, controls, a seek bar and volume.
- **A wide Now Playing.** Big artwork and controls on the left, lyrics or Up next on the right. Press Esc to close it.
- **YouTube Music.** Search, play and browse YouTube Music just like on your phone: songs, albums, artists, playlists and mixes. It uses the same YouTube Music engine as the phone app.
- **Sign in to YouTube Music.** A Google sign-in window opens where you can pick or add any Google account. Your likes, library and mixes come along.
- **Your styles.** The app style you pick in Settings shapes the desktop: the sidebar, the player bar and Home. Your player style shows up in Now Playing, with lyrics or Up next beside it.
- **Your music folders.** AlexLMP plays the music in your Music folder. Add more folders in Settings > Music folders.
- **Media keys and the Ubuntu top bar controls** work with AlexLMP.
- **Keyboard:** Space plays or pauses. Ctrl + Right and Ctrl + Left skip songs.

## Installing
Download the .deb and open it, or run `sudo apt install ./AlexLMP-linux-v0.1.0-amd64.deb` in the folder you downloaded it to.

If you enjoy AlexLMP, you can support the project from the card at the top of Settings <3
