All your music, one player. Now on Linux.

AlexLMP for Linux 0.1.0 is the first desktop version.

## What's here
- **Made for a computer.** A sidebar with Home, Mix, Library, a YouTube Music / This computer switch, your liked songs, pins, playlists and saved albums, and your account at the bottom. A search bar along the top (Ctrl + K jumps to it) with back and forward buttons, a Now playing panel on the right you can hide, and a floating player bar with volume.
- **A new Home.** A greeting, Jump back in, Quick picks in columns, Listen again, your mixes and more.
- **Artist, album and playlist pages for wide screens.** The artwork, details and buttons sit on the left, and the songs, albums and singles on the right. Everything opens inside the window, so the sidebar and the player bar stay put.
- **Now Playing with your player style.** The real AlexLMP player you picked, with the same buttons and look as on your phone, next to the lyrics. When a song has no lyrics you see what's up next instead. Press Esc to close it. Playing a song doesn't open it, so open it from the player bar when you want it.
- **YouTube Music.** Search, play and browse YouTube Music just like on your phone: songs, albums, artists, playlists and mixes.
- **Sign in to YouTube Music.** A Google sign-in window opens where you can pick or add any Google account. Your likes, library and mixes come along.
- **Your styles.** AlexLMP Classic is the default player and Adaptive (colors from the album art) is the default theme. Breathing artwork and word-by-word lyrics start off and can be turned on in Settings.
- **Your music folders.** AlexLMP plays the music in your Music folder. Add more folders in Settings > Music folders.
- **Media keys and the Ubuntu top bar controls** work with AlexLMP.
- **Keyboard:** Space plays or pauses, Ctrl + Right and Ctrl + Left skip songs, Ctrl + K searches. Typing in a search box never controls the music.

## Installing
Download the .deb and open it, or run `sudo apt install ./AlexLMP-linux-v0.1.0-amd64.deb` in the folder you downloaded it to.
