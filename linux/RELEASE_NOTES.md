All your music, one player. Now on Linux and Windows.

This is the first version of AlexLMP for computers.

## What you get
- **YouTube Music and your own music in one app.** Search, play and browse songs, albums, artists, playlists and mixes, or play the files in your Music folder.
- **Sign in with Google** to bring your likes, library and mixes along.
- **A layout built for a big screen.** Your library is always on the left, the player is always at the bottom, and pages open in the middle.
- **Lyrics next to the player.** If a song has no lyrics, you see what's playing next instead.
- **Your phone's styles.** The same player styles and themes as the phone app.
- **Keyboard and media keys.** Space plays or pauses, Ctrl + Left and Ctrl + Right skip, Ctrl + K searches.

## Installing
For Ubuntu, Debian, Mint and Pop!_OS: download the .deb and open it, or run `sudo apt install ./AlexLMP-linux-v0.1.0-amd64.deb`.

For Arch, Manjaro and EndeavourOS: download the .pkg.tar.zst and run `sudo pacman -U AlexLMP-linux-v0.1.0-x86_64.pkg.tar.zst`.

For Windows 10 and 11: download AlexLMP-windows-v0.1.0-setup.exe and run it. No admin rights needed. If Windows SmartScreen warns you, click More info, then Run anyway.
